### Local News, Media, and Behavioral Studies

For qualitative insights into inhabitant behavior (e.g., turnout, language preferences, social trends). These often reference official data.

- **Las Provincias and Levante-EMV**: Regional dailies covering Gandía; search archives for "encuestas Gandía" or "comportamiento electoral." Examples: 2023 general election analysis (PSOE outperformed polls locally).
  - Links: [Las Provincias](https://www.lasprovincias.es/), [Levante-EMV](https://www.levante-emv.com/)

- **ARA Newspaper**: Covers cultural/language polls (e.g., Mar 2025: 50.53% Valencian vs. 49.47% Castilian preference in schools, relevant to Gandía's bilingual context).
  - Link: [ARA Valencian Poll](https://en.ara.cat/society/50-53-of-families-choose-valencian-as-their-vehicular-language-compared-to-49-47-who-choose-castilian_1_5306343.html)

- **City Population and UrbiStat**: Aggregators of INE data with Gandía-specific visualizations (e.g., density 1,315/km², annual growth 1.9%). Useful for behavioral proxies like migration patterns.
  - Links: [City Population Gandía](https://www.citypopulation.de/en/spain/comunitatvalenciana/valencia/46131__gandia/), [UrbiStat](https://ugeo.urbistat.com/AdminStat/en/es/demografia/dati-sintesi/gandia/20250222/4)

### Academic and Research Sources

For deeper behavioral analysis (e.g., participation, urban equity).

- **ResearchGate and Frontiers Journals**: Papers on Valencian governance and health equity (e.g., 2024 study on neighborhood influences in Valencia metro area, including Gandía proxies).
  - Search: "Gandía inhabitant behavior" on [ResearchGate](https://www.researchgate.net/) or [Frontiers](https://www.frontiersin.org/).

- **Dyntra Transparency Index**: Ranks Gandía's openness (e.g., high scores on elected officials info); tracks public engagement trends.
  - Link: [Dyntra Gandía](https://www.dyntra.org/poi/ayuntamiento-de-gandia/)

### Tips for Access and Use
- Many sources are in Spanish; use translation tools if needed.
- For real-time polls, check aggregators like PolitPro or Wikipedia's opinion polling pages (e.g., 2023 local elections in Valencian Community).
- Combine quantitative (INE/elections) with qualitative (news/polls) for holistic behavior insights, like high turnout in small towns (e.g., 90% in 2019 vs. national 70%).
- If data is paywalled (e.g., Statista), alternatives like INE are free.
